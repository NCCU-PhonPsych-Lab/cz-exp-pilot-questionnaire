C. Other Slavic language experience

Questions 13–14 · Exposure before the recording and abilities recalled at that time

13.Hearing and speaking other Slavic languages before the pilot recording

Thinking back to the 12 months leading up to your pilot recording (12 August 2025 to 12 August 2026), how often did you (a) hear each language and (b) speak it yourself? Answer the two parts separately.

Hearing includes conversations, television, videos, and other spoken material, even when you did not understand it. Speaking means using the language yourself, including conversation or practice. Do not count reading or writing here. Give your best overall estimate, or choose “Cannot remember”.

Example: Slovak — heard weekly, spoke never; Polish — heard less than monthly, spoke never.

How to choose a frequency
Daily
Every day or almost every day.
Weekly
At least once a week, but not every day.
Monthly
At least once a month, but less than weekly.
Less than monthly
Some exposure or use, but less than once a month.
Never
None during this period.

Add languages other than Czech here. The same languages will appear automatically in Question 14. Removing an additional language removes its answers from both questions.

14.Understanding and speaking other Slavic languages at the time of the pilot recording

Thinking back to your pilot recording on 12 August 2026, how well could you (a) understand spoken language and (b) speak each language listed in Question 13? Rate these separately based on what you remember about that time, rather than your abilities today. You do not need to have studied a language formally to rate it.

Choose “Cannot remember” if you cannot recall, or “Prefer not to answer” if you do not wish to provide a rating.

Example: Slovak — understanding 8/10, speaking 2/10; Polish — understanding 2/10, speaking 0/10.
